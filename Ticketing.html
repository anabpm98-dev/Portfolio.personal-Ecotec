Ticketing and Event Access - Club Sport Emelec

Overview

Preparation and management of tickets, coupons and accreditations for sports and entertainment events.

My role

  • Producing and keeping inventory of tickets for sports events.
  • Mass printing of approved accreditations.
  • Generating and delivering courtesy tickets.
  • Running database queries.

What I learned

Over the years, I have learned that ticket production is a strategic and essential component of any business involved in organizing and managing high-attendance events, particularly in the sports industry.


Precision and attention to detail are crucial, as any production error can lead to access control issues and directly affect the customer experience. That is why I take pride in maintaining an error rate of less than 1%, achieved through a rigorous quality control process that identifies, separates, and discards defective materials, ensuring that only tickets meeting the required quality standards reach the point of sale.


My commitment is to deliver reliable results by prioritizing quality, accuracy, and efficiency at every stage of the process. If you are looking for results as close to perfection as possible, I am the right person to work with.
