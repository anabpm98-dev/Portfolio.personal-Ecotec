Access Control at Mass Events - Show593

Overview

Coordination of staff and entry flow for large public events, working with an event access and ticketing company.

My role

  • Calling and coordinating event staff.
  • Assigning tasks to each team member.
  • Coordinating the operation of the systems and ticket office area.

What I learned

Once event tickets have been produced, the next critical step is ensuring an efficient and secure access control process. This requires rigorous management to validate each ticket, reduce waiting times, and prevent long queues, providing attendees with a smooth, orderly, and hassle-free entry experience.


At Show593, my team and I make this possible through proprietary technology solutions developed in-house. Our systems incorporate validation mechanisms designed to help detect counterfeit tickets and quickly verify ticket validity, facilitating a continuous flow of attendees. These solutions are complemented by coordination with the event's security personnel, helping protect attendees and maintain order throughout the entry process.


We currently have experience managing access control for a variety of events. One of our longest-standing clients is the Asociación de Ganaderos del Litoral y Galápagos, the organization behind the "Expoferia Ganadera", an annual event held at the Jaime Nebot Velasco fairgrounds in Durán, Ecuador.


Our partnership with this organization dates back to 2019, demonstrating our long-term commitment to service quality, reliability, and transparency in every operation. We also offer competitive pricing, providing efficient solutions tailored to the needs of each organization.


If you are looking for a team that combines proprietary technology, operational experience, and a strong commitment to attendee safety, Show593 is ready to help you manage event access with efficiency, reliability, and professionalism.
